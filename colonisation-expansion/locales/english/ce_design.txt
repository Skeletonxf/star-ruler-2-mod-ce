S_ExperienceRate: Veterancy Rate
S_LOCAL_DEFENSE: Local Defense
STT_S_LOCAL_DEFENSE: Amount of local defense generation while in friendly space.
S_RAMP_UP_TIME: Ramp Up Time
S_COMBAT_TIME: Combat Time
STT_COMBAT_TIME: <<
	Total amount of supply capacity divided by supply used while firing all weapons in combat in minutes.

	Will underestimate combat time if the supply usage drops due to damage or supply is regenerated during combat.
	Will overestimate combat time on carriers as the support ships' combat use is not included in this calculation.
>>
STT_Experience_Rate: <<
	Amount of time required to go from 0 xp to a full level of veterancy from simulators while in combat.

	Other sources of xp such as killing enemy ships will speed this process up. Additional veterancy levels require increasingly more xp per levels already acquired and will take progressively longer.
>>
